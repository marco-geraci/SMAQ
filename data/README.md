## Data folder
Datasets used in SMAQ examples.

## Sources

Childhood Respiratory Disease Study. Data on lung function in children from East Boston, recorded in the 1970s. 

City Populations in West Virginia. Demographic data (2000 Census) on the population size in different West Virginia cities. Data source: https://www.census.gov.

EU Statistics on Income and Living Conditions. A harmonised survey providing information on income, poverty, and social exclusion across European countries. Data source: European Union Statistics on Income and Living Conditions (EU-SILC), https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/microdata/european-union-statistics-on-income-and-living-conditions.

Staghorn Corals. Ecological data used to study depth distributions and species-specific heterogeneity in marine environments. Data source: Muir et al. (2015), https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.3b568.

US Fatality Analysis Reporting System. A nationwide database maintained by the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), containing detailed information on fatal motor vehicle crashes in the United States. Data source: https://www.nhtsa.gov/research-data/fatality-analysis-reporting-system-fars.

US Integrated Public Use Microdata Series. Harmonised individual-level data on pre-tax wage and salary income, along with demographic and socio-economic characteristics, from US Census and American Community Survey (ACS) samples. Data source: Ruggles et al. (2025).

US National Center for Health Statistics on Births and Infant Mortality. Vital national statistics data providing detailed information on births and infant deaths in the United States, widely used in perinatal and population health research. Data source: National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS), http://www.cdc.gov/nchs.

World Bank Internet Adoption. Country-level panel data on internet usage rates over time. Data source: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS.
